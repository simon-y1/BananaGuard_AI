🧭 Handling and Prevention

Practical next steps for this prediction.

{% if prediction["prediction"] == "healthy" %}

How to handle it

  • Continue regular inspection of new and older leaves.
  • Remove badly damaged or dead leaves with clean tools.
  • Keep the plant well watered without leaving the soil waterlogged.

How to prevent disease

  • Use clean planting material and disinfect tools between plants.
  • Maintain good spacing, drainage, and airflow.
  • Check the underside of leaves regularly for early symptoms.
{% elif prediction["prediction"] == "cordana" %}

How to handle Cordana

  • Remove infected leaves and destroy them away from the plantation.
  • Disinfect cutting tools after each plant.
  • Improve airflow and avoid wetting leaves during irrigation.

How to prevent Cordana

  • Use healthy planting material and keep plants properly spaced.
  • Clear fallen infected leaves from around the plant.
  • Monitor during warm, humid, and rainy periods.
{% elif prediction["prediction"] == "panama" %}

How to handle Panama disease

  • Isolate suspected plants and avoid moving soil, water, or tools from them.
  • Remove and dispose of infected plants according to local agricultural guidance.
  • Do not replant susceptible bananas in contaminated soil.

How to prevent Panama disease

  • Use certified, disease-free planting material.
  • Clean footwear, tools, and equipment before entering other fields.
  • Use tolerant or resistant cultivars recommended for your area.
{% elif prediction["prediction"] == "sigatoka" %}

How to handle Sigatoka

  • Remove leaves with extensive lesions and dispose of them safely.
  • Reduce leaf wetness by improving drainage and avoiding overhead irrigation.
  • Ask a local agricultural expert about an approved fungicide program.

How to prevent Sigatoka

  • Maintain good spacing and airflow between plants.
  • Remove fallen infected leaves and control weeds around plants.
  • Scout regularly so treatment begins before severe leaf loss.
{% else %}

General handling and prevention

  • Separate the affected plant and inspect nearby plants.
  • Remove severely damaged leaves with disinfected tools.
  • Use clean planting material, good drainage, and adequate airflow.
  • Contact a local plant-health specialist for confirmation and treatment advice.
{% endif %}
